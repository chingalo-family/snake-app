# Snake App - Getting Started Guide

## Welcome! 🎮

Welcome to the modern Snake App! This guide will help you understand how to play, what features are available, and how to get the most enjoyment from the game.

## How to Play

### Basic Controls

#### Mobile/Touch Devices
- **Swipe Up**: Move snake upward
- **Swipe Down**: Move snake downward
- **Swipe Left**: Move snake left
- **Swipe Right**: Move snake right

#### Gameplay Rules
1. Control the snake to collect food items 🍎
2. Each food item increases your score
3. Snake grows longer with each food collected
4. Avoid hitting walls or your own body
5. Try to achieve the highest score possible!

## Game Features

### 🍎 Food System
- **Variety**: Different food items with varying point values
- **Animation**: Food items pulse to attract attention
- **Scoring**: Range from 10 to 90 points per item

### ⚡ Power-Ups (NEW!)
Power-ups randomly appear on the grid and provide temporary boosts:

#### Speed Boost ⚡
- Makes snake faster for 5 seconds
- Challenges your reflexes
- Higher risk, higher reward!

#### Shield 🛡️
- Protects from one collision
- Gives you a second chance
- Blue glow indicates active shield

#### Score Multiplier 💎
- Doubles your points for 8 seconds
- Perfect for combo chaining
- Purple glow effect

#### Slow Motion 🐌
- Slows game down for 6 seconds
- Great for tight situations
- Cyan glow effect

### 🔥 Combo System (NEW!)
Build combos by collecting food consecutively!

**Combo Tiers:**
- **3-4 Combo**: NICE (Green) - 1.3x multiplier
- **5-9 Combo**: GREAT (Yellow) - 1.5x multiplier
- **10-14 Combo**: SUPER (Red) - 2.0x multiplier
- **15-19 Combo**: EPIC (Orange) - 2.5x multiplier
- **20+ Combo**: LEGENDARY (Purple) - 3.0x+ multiplier

**Combo Tips:**
- Combo resets after 3 seconds without food
- Higher combos = exponentially higher scores
- Combine with Score Multiplier for massive points!

### 📊 Statistics Tracking
The game tracks your performance:
- **Score**: Total points earned
- **Food Collected**: Number of items eaten
- **Highest Combo**: Best combo streak achieved
- **Snake Length**: How long your snake grew

## Pro Tips & Strategies

### For Beginners 🌟
1. **Start Slow**: Focus on smooth, controlled movements
2. **Plan Ahead**: Think about where the snake will be in 2-3 moves
3. **Use Walls**: Move along walls for easier navigation
4. **Center Space**: Try to keep the center of the screen open

### For Intermediate Players 🎯
1. **Combo Focus**: Collect food quickly to build combos
2. **Power-Up Priority**: Go for Score Multiplier during combos
3. **Shield Safety**: Use Shield when navigating tight spaces
4. **Corner Technique**: Learn to navigate corners efficiently

### For Advanced Players 🏆
1. **Combo Chaining**: Maintain 10+ combos consistently
2. **Risk Management**: Know when to take risks for power-ups
3. **Speed Control**: Use Slow Motion strategically before tight turns
4. **Score Optimization**: Combine high-value food + combo + multiplier

### Score Optimization Formula
```
Maximum Points = High Value Food + LEGENDARY Combo + Score Multiplier
Example: 90 points × 3.0 (combo) × 2.0 (multiplier) = 540 points!
```
