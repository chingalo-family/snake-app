# Privacy Policy for Snake App

**Last Updated:** December 28, 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to Snake App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Snake App mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you register for an account or use certain features of the app, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Username**: A unique identifier you choose for your account
- **Full Name**: Your real name for account personalization
- **Email Address**: For account recovery and important communications
- **Phone Number**: Optional, for additional account security
- **Password**: Encrypted and stored securely for authentication

### Game Data

We collect information about your gameplay to provide game features and improve your experience:

- **Game Scores**: Your scores from each game session
- **Game Statistics**: Number of games played, best score, average score, highest combo
- **Snake Length**: Maximum snake length achieved
- **Level Progress**: Current game level
- **Leaderboard Rankings**: Your position on global and organizational leaderboards
- **Timestamps**: When games were played

### Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the app:

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version
- **App Version**: The version of Snake App you're using
- **Session Data**: App usage patterns and session duration
- **Error Logs**: Crash reports and diagnostic information

### Usage Data

- **Gameplay Preferences**: Sound settings, haptic feedback preferences
- **User Interactions**: Button clicks, navigation patterns within the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### Account Management
- Create and manage your user account
- Authenticate your identity when you log in
- Enable password reset and account recovery

### Game Features
- Save your game progress and scores
- Display your statistics and achievements
- Rank you on leaderboards (global and organizational)
- Sync your data across devices (if applicable)

### App Improvement
- Analyze app usage to improve features and user experience
- Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
- Develop new features based on user behavior
- Monitor app performance and stability

### Communication
- Send you important service announcements
- Respond to your support requests and feedback
- Notify you about app updates (if you've opted in)

### Competition and Social Features
- Enable competitive gameplay through leaderboards
- Display usernames and scores to other players in leaderboards
- Facilitate user rankings and comparisons

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

Some data is stored locally on your device using:

- **SQLite Database**: Game scores, user information, and offline data
- **Shared Preferences**: App settings and preferences (sound, haptic feedback)
- **Cache**: Temporary data for app performance

### Remote Storage

Your account information and game scores are synchronized with our DHIS2 backend server:

- Data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections
- User passwords are never stored in plain text on your device
- All server communications are authenticated

### Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data transmission uses industry-standard encryption protocols
- **Authentication**: Password-protected user authentication
- **Access Control**: Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only
- **Regular Updates**: We keep our security measures up to date

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Information We Share

We do **NOT** sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

#### Within Leaderboards
- Your **username** and **game scores** are visible to other users in leaderboards
- Your **ranking** and **statistics** may be displayed in competitive features
- This is essential for the competitive gameplay experience

#### Service Providers
- We use DHIS2 platform for backend services and data management
- Email service providers (for sending password reset emails and support communications)
- These providers are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential

#### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:
- Valid legal processes (court orders, subpoenas)
- Protection of our legal rights
- Prevention of fraud or security threats
- Compliance with applicable laws and regulations

### Information We Do NOT Share
- We do not share your email address publicly
- We do not share your phone number with other users
- We do not share your password with anyone
- We do not sell your data to advertisers or marketing companies

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

### DHIS2 Platform
- Backend infrastructure for user management and data storage
- Subject to their own privacy policies and security measures

### Email Service (Mailer)
- Used for sending password reset emails and support communications
- Configured with secure SMTP connections

### Flutter/Dart Packages
The app uses various open-source packages for functionality:
- **audioplayers**: Background music and sound effects
- **haptic_feedback**: Vibration feedback
- **shared_preferences**: Local settings storage
- **sqflite**: Local database
- **http**: Network communications

These packages do not collect or transmit your personal data independently.

## Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### Access and Portability
- View your account information in the Profile section
- Request a copy of your data by contacting support

### Correction
- Update your profile information through the app's Profile settings
- Change your password using the Change Password feature

### Deletion
- Request account deletion by contacting our support team
- Note: Leaderboard scores may be retained for historical integrity

### Opt-Out
- Disable sound effects in Settings
- Disable haptic feedback in Settings
- Unsubscribe from optional email communications

### Data Retention
- Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: Personal information deleted within 30 days
- Leaderboard data may be anonymized rather than deleted to maintain historical records
