# Privacy Policy - Quick Summary

*Last Updated: December 28, 2024*

## What We Collect

**Your Account:**
- Username, name, email, and phone number (optional)
- Encrypted password for secure login

**Your Gameplay:**
- Game scores, statistics, and rankings
- Game level and progress
- Leaderboard positions

**App Settings:**
- Sound and haptic feedback preferences
- Device information for app functionality

## How We Use It

✓ Save your game progress and scores  
✓ Show you on leaderboards  
✓ Improve the app and fix bugs  
✓ Send important account notifications  
✓ Provide customer support

## What We Share

**Leaderboards:** Your username and scores are visible to other players  
**Service Providers:** We use DHIS2 for data storage (secure and encrypted)  
**Legal Requirements:** Only if required by law

**We NEVER:**
- Sell your data to anyone
- Share your email publicly
- Give your password to anyone

## Your Rights

You can:
- View and update your profile
- Change your password anytime
- Turn off sounds and haptic feedback
- Request account deletion
- Contact us with privacy concerns

## Security

- All data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS)
- Passwords are never stored in plain text
- Local data is securely stored on your device
- Regular security updates
