# Full release 1.4 — Bonus critter and growing walls

**Intended version:** `1.4.0`  
**Depends on:** 1.2 Challenges (growing walls as a challenge); bonus may appear in campaign  
**Detail:** [plans/03-nokia-and-arcade-inspired.md](../plans/03-nokia-and-arcade-inspired.md) · [plans/02-google-snake-inspired.md](../plans/02-google-snake-inspired.md) § Growing walls  
**Experience gap:** food is always static; Maze is only **pre-placed** rocks

## Player experience

- **Bonus critter:** after every M common eats, a **short-lived high-value** animal appears with a small timer ring. Detour for points or skip. Same collectible look/SFX family as today.
- **Growing walls (Rising stumps):** a **named challenge** — extra blockers appear as you eat. Looks like Maze stones, **different chip** so campaign Maze is not a surprise.

Static **Maze** / **Wrap maze** campaign levels stay as in 1.1 unless a catalog row is explicitly updated.

## UX

| Surface | Change |
|---------|--------|
| Campaign (optional) | Bonus spawn on selected levels; HUD ring only while bonus is alive. No new buttons. |
| Challenges | **Rising stumps** card: chip, tip (“New rocks appear as you eat”), Classic walls, spacious grid. |
| Board | Bonus uses existing rarity art; blockers use existing maze materials. |
| First-run | One-line tip on the challenge card, same pattern as Wrap. |

## Modes

- Bonus: modifier on Classic / Wrap / selected campaign levels.  
- Growing walls: **not** a rename of Maze. Maze = static seed; Rising stumps = spawn over time with a safe radius around the head.

## Must not change

Maze chip meaning; levels 1–12 rules; Sprint/Zen/Collector from 1.2–1.3.

## Out of scope (1.4)

Eat-all Nibbler maze, vine trail, replacing Maze generation.

## Acceptance

1. Bonus never spawns on snake, food, or closed pockets.
2. Growing-wall cells never occupy the snake or food; head is not trapped on spawn (taxicab radius).
3. Food never on blockers.
4. Players can tell Maze vs Rising stumps **before** Start.
5. EN + SW; unit tests for spawn cadence and exclusion.

## Tests to add

- Bonus spawn/despawn/score tier.
- Growing wall count increases on eat cadence; no spawn on occupied cells.
